# Demo Store Business Facts

## Return and Exchange Policy
Demo Store accepts returns or exchanges within 7 days of delivery when the item is unused, undamaged, and returned with its original packaging. Final approval is subject to store review. Used or damaged items are not eligible unless the item arrived defective.

## Delivery
Orders are dispatched after confirmation. Delivery time depends on destination and courier availability.

## Payment Methods
Demo Store accepts Cash on Delivery, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Bank Transfer.

## Support
Customers may contact the store team for order, return, delivery, or payment questions that require manual review.
