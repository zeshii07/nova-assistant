# Family-level filtered browsing (v2.10)

Generic family/category requests carrying attributes no longer auto-select the best fuzzy product match.

Examples:
- `can i get black shoes` -> browse Footwear filtered to Black
- `show me navy shirts` -> browse shirt family filtered to Navy
- `i want black running shoes` -> specific Running Shoes selection

The Playground also includes Healthcare and Education semantic-demo tenants. They exercise the same tenant, domain-schema, Conversation Intelligence, Assistant, CRM, Humanization and Replay path. Transactional healthcare/education capabilities are intentionally not implemented in this patch.
