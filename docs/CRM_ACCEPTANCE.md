# CRM Acceptance Criteria

- Official customer records are isolated by tenant and customer.
- CRM and AI memory remain separate.
- Capability-scoped permissions are enforced.
- Notes, tags, custom fields, and activities are supported.
- CRM events are versioned.
- The assistant records interactions and can personalize greetings from CRM.
- Existing Sprint 1–3.5 regression tests continue to pass.
