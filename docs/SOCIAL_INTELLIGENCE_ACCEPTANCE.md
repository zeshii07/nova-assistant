# Social Intelligence Acceptance

The release is accepted when:

- mixed social + business messages preserve the business task;
- `other shoes` reopens category choices;
- unsupported products stay inside Catalog and receive a friendly reply;
- unsupported business domains are rejected based on tenant capabilities;
- small talk such as `how do you do today` is handled conversationally;
- checkout interruptions remain safe and resumable;
- all inherited tests and conversation compliance datasets pass.
